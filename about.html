About Us

A centre built on faith, and finished by the community

The story of St. Francis Kijaguzo Health Centre III — from one priest's idea to a facility trusted across Nakaseke District.

Home / About

Our Mission

Compassion, Care, Healing, Hope

St. Francis Kijaguzo Health Centre III exists to bring accessible, high-quality healthcare to the people of Nakaseke District — delivered in the spirit of compassion at the heart of the Kasana–Luweero Diocese.

Our History

From a parish idea to a district health centre

The Beginning

A parish priest's vision

The idea for a health facility in Kijaguzo was first initiated by parish priest Rev. Fr. Charles Ssenkungu, who began construction with the support of local parishioners.

Along the Way

Limited finances, undeterred faith

With funding stretched thin, Fr. Ssenkungu was unable to complete the project alone — but the work he started did not stop there.

Taking the Baton

Caritas Kasana–Luweero steps in

Caritas Kasana–Luweero took over the project and secured the donor support needed to see it through, partnering with German organisations ZFK e.V. and BMZ.

2012

Doors open to the community

St. Francis Kijaguzo Health Centre III was completed and formally opened by the Rt. Rev. Bishop Paul Semogerere of the Kasana–Luweero Diocese — and has been serving patients ever since.

Why Kijaguzo

Healthcare grounded in trust

01

Rooted in Community

Founded by a local parish priest and built with the support of parishioners — this Centre has always belonged to Kijaguzo first.

02

Guided by Faith

Operated under the Kasana–Luweero Diocese and Caritas, care here is offered in a spirit of compassion, not just treatment.

03

Open to All

While Nakaseke District is home base, the Centre regularly welcomes and treats patients from surrounding districts too.
