What We Offer

Care for every stage of life

From routine check-ups to maternity care and diagnostics — fifteen services across four areas of care, extended primarily to Nakaseke District and beyond.

General Medicine

Everyday diagnosis and treatment for common illness and ongoing conditions.

Maternity Services

Antenatal care, safe delivery and postnatal support for mothers and newborns.

Laboratory Services

Diagnostic testing and analysis to support accurate treatment decisions.

Immunization

Routine vaccination programmes for infants, children and expectant mothers.

Inpatient Care

Overnight and extended care for patients who need closer monitoring.
