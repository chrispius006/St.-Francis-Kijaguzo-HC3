Education & Training

Growing knowledge alongside good health

Beyond treatment, the Centre invests in health education for the community and clinical training for the next generation of caregivers.

Home / Education & Training

Our Programmes

How We Educate & Train

Community Health Education

Health talks and awareness sessions delivered during outreach visits and at the Centre, covering topics from maternal health to disease prevention.

Clinical Attachments & Internships

Placements for nursing and clinical medicine students to gain hands-on experience under the supervision of our staff.

Staff Continuing Development

Ongoing professional training for Centre staff, keeping clinical knowledge and practice current.

Family & Maternal Health Sessions

Guided sessions for expectant and new mothers on antenatal care, natural family planning and newborn health.

Who It's For

Education for the Whole Community

01

Patients & Families

Practical, plain-language health education delivered alongside routine care and outreach visits.

02

Students & Trainees

Structured attachments for nursing and clinical students from partner training institutions.

03

Our Own Staff

Regular refreshers and skills training to keep the whole team's practice sharp and current.
