Services

Fifteen services, four areas of care

The Centre extends a wide range of health services primarily to residents of Nakaseke District, and regularly welcomes patients travelling from beyond it.

Home / Services
01

General & Clinical Care

Everyday diagnosis, admission and treatment for the community's most common health needs.

General Medicine

Diagnosis and treatment for everyday illness and ongoing conditions.

Outpatient

Same-day consultation and treatment without an overnight stay.

Inpatient

Admission and overnight care for patients who need closer monitoring.

Minor Surgical Procedures

Small-scale surgical care handled safely on site.

Referrals

Connecting patients to specialist facilities when a case needs it.

02

Maternal & Reproductive Health

Supporting mothers and families from pregnancy through delivery and beyond.

Antenatal Care

Regular check-ups to monitor the health of mother and baby during pregnancy.

Maternity Services

Safe delivery and postnatal support for mothers and newborns.

Natural Family Planning

Guidance on fertility awareness and family planning methods.

03

Diagnostics

Testing and screening that gives patients and clinicians clear answers, faster.

Laboratory Services

Diagnostic testing and analysis to support accurate treatment decisions.

Ultrasound

Imaging used to check on pregnancy progress and internal health.

HIV Testing & Counselling

Confidential testing paired with supportive, judgement-free counselling.

04

Community & Prevention

Taking care beyond the Centre's walls, out into the villages of Nakaseke District.

Immunization

Routine vaccination programmes for infants, children and expectant mothers.

Community Counselling

Confidential guidance and support for individuals and families.

Community Health Insurance

Helping local families access affordable, prepaid healthcare cover.

Community Outreaches

Bringing screening, education and basic care directly to nearby villages.

Ready When You Are

Need care, or still have questions?

Walk in during opening hours, or reach out ahead of your visit to plan a maternity, lab or outreach appointment.
